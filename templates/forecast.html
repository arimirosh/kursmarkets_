Forecast setup

Step 1

Market encoding

The app loads aligned close-price series for the target and related MOEX assets.

Step 2

Cross-market synergy

Returns from related assets become external signals for the target stock.

Step 3

Movement regression

A ridge model predicts the next target return from the latest market state.

Step 4

Pseudo-volatility

Recent volatility and model noise are converted into a forecast confidence score.
